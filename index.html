About S for Stays

Practical Hospitality Designed Around Your Stay

S for Stays offers a straightforward, welcoming stay experience in Pakistan. Located on a main boulevard with free parking, our property features seven rooms arranged across the second and third floors—comprising five Studio Rooms and two 1-Bedroom Rooms.

Every room is prepared with clean beds, a good washroom with hot and cold water, free Wi-Fi, and electricity backup. Instead of complicated checkout screens, you can browse each individual room below, select your preferred dates, and send your inquiry directly to our team on WhatsApp for prompt confirmation of availability and rates.
